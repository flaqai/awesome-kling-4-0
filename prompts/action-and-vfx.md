# Action, Sports & VFX Prompts

[← Catalog](README.md) · [Prompting guide](../docs/PROMPT-GUIDE.md)

Four original Kling prompts built around spatial geography, weight, cause-and-effect and readable VFX. Complex action becomes more stable when the prompt separates subject, camera and environment motion.

## 1. The Glass Manta Pursuit

![Glass manta salt-canyon pursuit](../assets/images/prompt-fantasy-salt-canyon.png)

**Use case:** science-fantasy chase · **Format:** 12s, 16:9 or 21:9 · **Best mode:** start image + custom multi-shot

**Why it works:** The rider, skimmer, creature, slope and camera each receive a coherent path; no beat contains more than one stunt.

```text
[OUTPUT]
12 seconds, ultra-wide science-fantasy pursuit, photorealistic materials, three shots, fast but readable action, no weapons.

[CONTINUITY ANCHORS]
Courier Nara: adult rider, sand-colored protective suit, compact backpack, opaque bronze visor.
Skimmer: one-seat rectangular solar skimmer, weathered cream shell, two dark solar panels on its nose, no wheels or logo.
Sky creature: colossal translucent manta-shaped cloud organism, glasslike membrane with slow internal light currents.
Preserve all designs, scale relationships and travel direction left-to-right.

[WORLD]
Pale salt canyon after a storm. Hard slope descends left-to-right toward mirrored pools. Late sun frame left; wind carries loose crystalline dust downhill.

[TIMED SHOTS]
0–4s — Low parallel tracking shot on Nara’s left side, camera and skimmer moving downhill at equal speed. Skimmer banks 20 degrees into one right curve; Nara leans with it. The rear edge throws one continuous dust ribbon that follows the curve and settles under gravity.
4–8s — Wide aerial trailing shot, never crossing the travel axis. The glass manta glides overhead in the same direction but slower, casting a broad moving shadow that overtakes Nara. Its wings complete one heavy downstroke; canyon dust reacts half a second later.
8–12s — Front three-quarter low shot moving backward at matched speed. A narrow salt ridge breaks ahead. Nara pulls the skimmer nose upward and clears one two-meter gap; suspension compresses visually on landing through a brief body dip and dense dust burst. The manta passes above, filling the sky as camera holds horizon level.

[AUDIO]
Steady electric turbine whine, granular salt hiss and strong crosswind. Wing downstroke produces a deep airy pressure pulse at 6s. Landing has one low impact plus settling crystals. No dialogue or music.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Skimmer remains in ground effect, never becomes an aircraft. Rider stays seated with both hands on controls. Physically delayed dust and shadow reaction. One gap, one jump, one creature. No collision, explosion, weapon, duplicate vehicle, changing visor, impossible camera teleport, backwards dust, logos, text or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** salt canyon → ice shelf / red clay quarry / flooded rice terrace; manta → cloud whale / ribbon flock.

**Failure check:** Watch frame-by-frame through the landing; hands, controls, skimmer geometry and shadow should remain connected.

## 2. The Last Hold

**Use case:** indoor climbing sports commercial · **Format:** 10s, 9:16 · **Best mode:** athlete reference + single take

**Why it works:** A single continuous route and three contact points make athletic movement easier to judge.

```text
[OUTPUT]
10 seconds, 9:16, one continuous indoor bouldering take, grounded sports realism, energetic but not superhuman.

[ATHLETE LOCK]
Sam: adult climber, lean build, short dark hair, teal sleeveless top, charcoal climbing pants, chalked hands, no jewelry. Preserve body proportions and clothing.

[ROUTE GEOMETRY]
Overhanging matte-gray wall. Start position: left foot on blue foothold, right foot on lower black volume, left hand on blue side-pull, right hand free. Final bright orange hold is 70 centimeters above and right. Thick crash mat below.

[CAMERA AND ACTION]
Camera begins low behind Sam’s right shoulder, then cranes upward only as Sam rises, constant distance, no cuts.
0–3s — Sam looks at the orange hold, exhales, shifts hips left while keeping three existing contacts. Chalk dust falls naturally.
3–6s — Right foot moves once to a higher blue foothold and settles fully before the next action. Sam drives through legs; torso rises close to wall.
6–8s — Right hand reaches in one clean arc and catches the orange hold. Fingers close; shoulder absorbs the swing. Feet stay on their holds.
8–10s — Sam controls the pendulum, brings left hand to match the orange hold, then looks down with a relieved grin.

[AUDIO]
Rubber shoe squeak, breath, fabric stretch, soft gym room tone and one small audience gasp at the catch. No dialogue, announcer or music.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Correct contact sequence and realistic gravity. No free-floating pause, limb duplication, hold movement, wall texture shift, impossible reach, foot sliding, sudden camera cut, crowd entering frame, logo, score text or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** bouldering → parkour precision jump / skateboard manual / fencing lunge; keep one decisive move.

**Failure check:** Each hand and foot must attach to a visible hold; if any contact is ambiguous, simplify the camera.

## 3. Paper City Awakens

**Use case:** start-to-end material transformation · **Format:** 10s, 16:9 · **Best mode:** start + end frames

**Why it works:** The transformation follows a single expanding wave and preserves landmarks rather than changing everything at once.

```text
[OUTPUT]
10 seconds, 16:9, one continuous tabletop macro transformation from paper model to living miniature city, tactile magical realism.

[START FRAME]
Open cream sketchbook on a dark wooden desk. An intricate white paper city rises from the center fold: one domed station at left, narrow canal center, three stepped houses right, single paper tree foreground. Adult hand rests outside frame right holding a wooden pencil.

[END FRAME]
Same camera angle and exact city layout, now a living miniature at blue hour: station windows glow, canal contains moving water, houses are painted muted terracotta, tree has green leaves. Sketchbook and desk remain visible, proving the scale.

[TRANSFORMATION]
0–2s — Pencil tip touches the canal line at the bottom of frame. A thin cobalt ink current flows forward along the drawn channel.
2–7s — Wherever the current passes, transformation happens once and only once: paper canal becomes reflective water; adjacent buildings gain texture and color from bottom upward; flat cutout windows illuminate; paper tree gently unfolds leaves. The wave travels foreground to background at constant speed.
7–10s — Current reaches the station. One miniature tram exits slowly and stops before the canal bridge. Camera makes a five-centimeter push-in; human hand and desk stay still.

[AUDIO]
Pencil scratch, paper unfolding, tiny water trickle, distant miniature bell at 8s. A soft room tone remains to preserve real-world scale. No narration or music.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Preserve the exact map, landmark positions, sketchbook fold and camera angle. Transformation travels only behind the ink front; no explosions, smoke, loose pages, full-size city illusion or new landmarks. One tram only. No readable text, logo or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** city → garden / train world / ocean map; ink wave → stitched thread / morning shadow / spilled light.

**Failure check:** Overlay first and final frames mentally—the geometry must match even though materials change.

## 4. Storm in a Teacup

**Use case:** macro weather spectacle · **Format:** 8s, 1:1 · **Best mode:** start image + single take

**Why it works:** A spectacular event stays bounded inside one object with an explicit energy decay.

```text
[OUTPUT]
8 seconds, 1:1, one locked macro shot, photorealistic tabletop surrealism, physically coherent miniature storm.

[SCENE]
Plain white porcelain teacup on a worn oak table beside a closed book. Tea surface visible from a high three-quarter angle. Warm afternoon window light from frame left; cup handle points right. No branding or text.

[ACTION TIMELINE]
0–2s — Tea is almost still. One tiny circular cloud forms two centimeters above the liquid, rotating clockwise. The room outside the cup remains calm.
2–5s — Cloud darkens and releases localized rain only into the cup. Concentric ripples spread to the porcelain edge. Three miniature lightning filaments illuminate the tea from within, each separated by half a second; no bolt leaves the cup.
5–7s — A thumb-sized waterspout rises from the center and rotates once, pulling three loose tea leaves into orbit without spilling liquid over the rim.
7–8s — Storm collapses into one soft puff; surface settles toward stillness. A final single droplet falls into the exact center for a loopable end.

[AUDIO]
Quiet room, magnified rain taps, three delicate electrical ticks synchronized to light, low water swirl, final droplet. No thunder boom, dialogue or music.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Cup, handle, book, table, camera and room light remain fixed. Weather is contained entirely within cup boundaries. Realistic reflections and ripple propagation. No overflow, broken cup, fire, giant ocean, camera movement, text, logo or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** teacup storm → snow globe aurora / soup-bowl monsoon / perfume-bottle desert.

**Failure check:** Ensure storm scale remains miniature and the background never reacts as if full-size weather entered the room.
