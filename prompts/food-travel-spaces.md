# Food, Travel, Architecture & Craft Prompts

[← Catalog](README.md) · [Prompting guide](../docs/PROMPT-GUIDE.md)

Four original Kling prompts for sensory detail, destination identity, interiors and making processes. Keep food handling safe and represent real places and crafts accurately.

## 1. Scallion Pancake ASMR

**Use case:** food process / ASMR · **Format:** 12s, 9:16 · **Best mode:** start image + macro multi-shot

**Why it works:** Each food transformation has a visible tool action and its own synchronized sound.

```text
[OUTPUT]
12 seconds, 9:16, three-shot food ASMR, natural kitchen light, honest texture, no presenter face.

[FOOD AND HAND LOCK]
One round layered scallion pancake, 16 centimeters wide, pale dough with evenly distributed green scallion rings. Adult cook’s clean hands, short natural nails, dark-blue apron. Black carbon-steel pan and one flat wooden spatula.

[WORLD]
Small home kitchen beside an open window, soft morning side light, pale stone counter. Background remains quiet and out of focus.

[TIMED SHOTS]
0–4s — Top-down macro. Cook rolls the layered dough forward exactly once; visible scallion rings compress but stay inside the dough. Fine flour shifts outward under the rolling pin. Audio emphasizes wood on dough and dry flour.
4–8s — Side macro at pan height. Pancake lies flat in a thin film of oil. Edges bubble and turn golden from left to right. Wooden spatula lifts one edge to reveal crisp concentric layers, then lowers it; no flip occurs in this shot.
8–12s — 45-degree close-up on cutting board. Cook makes one clean center cut with a broad knife, then gently separates the two halves by two centimeters. Steam escapes and flaky layers open. Hold for one second on texture.

[AUDIO]
No music or speech. Rolling friction, quiet window birds, controlled oil sizzle, spatula scrape, one crisp knife contact and delicate crackle as layers separate. Audio perspective matches each macro shot.

[CONSTRAINTS]
One pancake, one pan, one spatula, one knife. Correct tool contact and adult hands. Food does not regenerate, jump position or burn. No excessive oil splash, floating steam, raw center after cooked exterior, extra fingers, text, packaging, logo or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** pancake → tortilla / roti / crêpe / flatbread; focus on one authentic process and revise tools accordingly.

**Failure check:** Confirm the pancake’s size, scallion pattern and cooked state progress logically across cuts.

## 2. One Morning in Quanzhou

**Use case:** destination short film · **Format:** 15s, 16:9 · **Best mode:** location references + multi-shot

**Why it works:** Specific everyday sensory moments avoid a generic “travel montage,” while the narration stays factual and modest.

```text
[OUTPUT]
15 seconds, 16:9, four-shot dawn travel poem in Quanzhou, Fujian, observational realism, warm humid atmosphere.

[LOCATION ANCHORS]
Historic arcaded street with weathered cream walls and dark timber shutters; small tea stall with red clay kettle; local harbor with low working boats. Respect real southern Fujian architectural scale. No invented landmark mashups or readable commercial signs.

[TIMED SHOTS]
0–4s — Locked wide under an arcade just before sunrise. Shopkeeper lifts one wooden shutter; bicycle passes slowly through the pool of warm interior light. Damp stone reflects the movement.
4–8s — Macro at tea stall. Hot water pours from red clay kettle into one small white cup; camera racks focus to steam catching sideways sun. Adult customer’s hand receives the cup from below.
8–12s — Medium tracking shot beside an adult woman walking through the arcade with a woven market basket. Camera keeps pace; hanging fabric moves in the sea breeze; she nods to an off-screen neighbor.
12–15s — Wide harbor reveal. Camera moves out from behind a stone column to show morning boats in soft haze. One small gull crosses; hold a calm final frame.

[AUDIO]
Continuous distant bicycle bells, early conversation and humid city room tone; kettle pour close at 5s; harbor engine enters gradually. Mandarin voice-over, calm and unhurried: “清晨先从一扇窗、一杯茶，慢慢醒来。” No song.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Natural local daily life without costume spectacle or exoticization. Preserve sunrise direction and humid color across shots. No crowd duplication, fake script, giant temple inserted into street, drone hyperlapse, logo, subtitle or watermark. Voice-over remains off-screen; no visible mouth moves.
```

**Swap ideas:** replace with a place you know well; use three verifiable local details, one resident action and one honest ambient sound.

**Failure check:** Ask a local reviewer to verify architecture, clothing, behavior, language and narration before publishing.

## 3. Courtyard Light Study

**Use case:** boutique hotel / real-estate film · **Format:** 12s, 16:9 · **Best mode:** room references + single take

**Why it works:** A single motivated camera path proves spatial relationships and avoids impossible room morphing.

```text
[OUTPUT]
12 seconds, 16:9, one continuous architectural walkthrough, refined but truthful hospitality film, no people.

[SPACE LOCK]
Compact lime-plaster guest suite around a planted courtyard. Entry door opens to sitting area; low linen sofa frame left; courtyard centered behind sliding glass; sleeping alcove frame right behind timber screen. Small circular reflecting pool in courtyard; one olive tree. Preserve exact plan, furniture count and material colors.

[CAMERA PATH]
Camera at adult eye height begins just inside the entry, gimbal-smooth but human pace. Move forward 2.5 meters through sitting area, arc gently right around the sofa without passing through it, pause to frame courtyard, then slide another meter right to reveal sleeping alcove through the timber screen. Horizon stays level; lens feels 24–28mm without extreme distortion.

[ENVIRONMENT MOTION]
Late-afternoon sun enters from courtyard and projects stable leaf shadows onto lime wall. One curtain edge moves in a light breeze. Reflecting-pool ripples respond to a single falling olive leaf at 7s. Bedding, furniture and doors remain still.

[AUDIO]
Soft room tone, distant birds, quiet fabric movement, one water drop and subtle footsteps from the camera operator. No music, narration or artificial whoosh.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Architecturally possible continuous path. No wall, doorway, furniture, pool or alcove changes; no wide-angle stretching, room expansion, invisible cut, floating décor, overexposed window, fireplace, TV, text, logo or watermark. Show only spaces actually connected in the declared plan.
```

**Swap ideas:** hotel room → apartment / restaurant / studio / exhibition; redraw the plan and camera path first.

**Failure check:** Trace the camera path on a floor plan; if it crosses furniture or a wall, revise the prompt before generation.

## 4. Blue Pottery Kiln

**Use case:** craft-process documentary · **Format:** 15s, 16:9 · **Best mode:** artisan + vessel references

**Why it works:** Tool contact and material state evolve chronologically, while safety and cultural specificity remain explicit.

```text
[OUTPUT]
15 seconds, 16:9, four-shot observational craft documentary, natural workshop light, respectful and unsentimental.

[ARTISAN AND OBJECT LOCK]
Farah: adult ceramic artisan, medium-brown skin, dark hair covered by plain indigo cotton scarf, clay-gray apron, clear safety glasses during kiln shot.
Vessel: one low round vase with narrow neck, carved wave band around shoulder, cobalt-blue glaze. Preserve vessel proportions and carved motif.

[TIMED SHOTS]
0–4s — Close side shot at pottery wheel. Farah’s wet hands center one clay form; wheel rotates counterclockwise at steady speed. Fingers press symmetrically and neck rises two centimeters.
4–8s — Macro insert on leather-hard vase. Farah uses one small wooden loop tool to carve the wave band in a continuous line; clay ribbon curls away under the tool.
8–11s — Medium shot at glazing bench. She pours cobalt glaze over the stationary vase once; excess drains into basin. Hands rotate the foot ring only after pouring stops.
11–15s — Kiln reveal after firing, with Farah wearing safety glasses and heat-resistant gloves. She opens an already cooled kiln slowly, lifts the finished blue vase with both hands and turns it 20 degrees toward window light to reveal the wave relief.

[AUDIO]
Wheel hum, wet clay, carving scrape, glaze pour and workshop birds. At final shot, room tone becomes quiet enough to hear gloves touch ceramic. No narration or music.

[CONSTRAINTS]
Chronological material states: wet clay → leather-hard carving → glazed unfired → cooled fired vessel. Safe kiln behavior; no visible flame or bare-hand hot handling. Preserve Farah and vessel motif. No magical instant firing, changing wheel direction, extra tools, costume styling, text, logo or watermark.
```

**Swap ideas:** pottery → glassblowing / bookbinding / weaving / instrument repair; research the actual process and PPE.

**Failure check:** Have a practitioner verify tool choice, material state, hand placement and safety sequence.
